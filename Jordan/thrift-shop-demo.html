How this works

Measured, not sized

Old sizing lies. Every ticket carries real measurements in centimetres so you can check against something you already own.

Graded honestly

A means barely worn. B means worn with no flaws. C means worn with a flaw, and the flaw is written on the ticket.

Held for 15 minutes

Adding to your bag takes the piece off the rail while you decide. Nobody else can buy it. Let it lapse and it goes back up.

Collect at the market, free

Buy online, choose the next market at checkout, and pick it up at the stall. No courier fee and you can try it on there.
