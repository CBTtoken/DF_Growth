# Tester brief, The Board and messaging

Two messages. Send the first to everybody. Send the second only to the one or two testers who are also KatisoBiz members and willing to spend a bit longer.

Before you send either: put five or six real posts up, add a WhatsApp number to the business you post as, and check the board does not look empty.

---

## MESSAGE 1: everybody (about 10 minutes)

```
Good day [Name],

I need about 10 minutes of your help testing something new we have built. It is not public yet, so please do not forward the link.

https://growth.digitalflyersa.co.za/board

On your phone, in this order:

1. Open the link. Scroll down and tap "Put The Board on your phone" so you get an icon like a normal app. On iPhone tap "Show me how" and follow the three steps.

2. Close it and open it again from that new icon.

3. Have a look around. Try the search box, and try the Area and Category dropdowns at the top.

4. Tap on any post to open it.

5. Tap "Share on WhatsApp" and send that post to me. I want to see how the preview looks coming out of your phone.

6. Tap the heart.

7. Tap "Click here to comment" and ask a question the way a real customer would. It only asks your name. No password, no code, no signup. Your comment is public, so keep it real.

8. Tap "Click here to reply" under somebody else's comment and add something.

9. Tap "Review" and leave a star rating.

10. Tap "Message ... directly". A chat screen opens like WhatsApp. Send me a message. I will reply, and you will get an email plus it appears under your Messages icon.

11. Go back to the board and tap "New post". Put up something you are actually looking for, or something you want to sell.

Then tell me:

- Anything confusing, slow, or that looked broken
- Did anything ask you for something you did not want to give
- Did the WhatsApp share look right when it arrived
- Would you actually use this to find a local business

Thank you, your honest answer helps more than a polite one.
```

## What to watch for while they test

Things worth chasing if a tester mentions them:

- **The share card arriving as a plain link** instead of a card with the business name and price on it. That means the preview did not load and I want to know which phone and which app.
- **Being asked for a password or a code anywhere.** There should be none. If somebody hits one, that is a bug and I need the screen.
- **A comment or post that does not appear.** Anything with a link in it is held on purpose, and the person is told so. Anything else is a bug.
- **The quote opening empty.** That means the KatisoBiz account is not linked to the Growth business.
- **Anything at all on the first screen.** If a tester says they had to scroll before they understood what the page was, tell me. That is the thing we have been fighting all week.

## What is expected, and is not a bug

- **A comment containing a link is held.** By design, and the person is told.
- **The unread badge does not change while you sit still.** It is correct every time a page loads or you navigate. Live updates need a piece we have not built.
- **A "Looking for" post disappears after 10 days.** By design. Business posts stay.
- **No "verified" badge anywhere.** There is no verification mechanism yet, and inventing a badge would be dishonest.
