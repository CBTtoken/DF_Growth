## Section 1: Let us get the suspicious part out of the way

You are right to be careful. So here is everything, up front.

**It costs nothing to join.** No sign-up fee, no starter pack, no monthly fee. If you are ever asked to pay us to be an agent, it is not us.

**There is no stock.** You are not buying products and reselling them. There is nothing sitting in your garage.

**You do not recruit other agents.** There is no downline, no team under you, no earning off other people's work. You earn on businesses you personally bring in. That is the only way to earn here.

**You are not employed by us.** You are independent. You choose your own hours, you work how you want, and you handle your own tax. We do not pay you a salary and we do not promise you will earn anything.

**We cannot guarantee you income.** What you make depends entirely on how many businesses you bring in. Some agents will make very little. We would rather say that now than have you find out later.

## Section 2: What you are actually selling

Most small businesses in South Africa have a Facebook page and nothing else. No proper website, nothing that shows up when someone searches, no way for a customer to find them at eleven at night when they are looking for a plumber.

DigitalFlyer SA fixes that. A professional page, built for them in days. A place on our marketplace where local customers search. Branded social media posts made for them every month. Real customer reviews.

Your job is the introduction. You do not build anything, you do not write anything, and you do not do the support. We do all of it. You find the business and make the connection.

## Section 3: What you earn

You earn a share of what the business pays, and you earn it again every year they renew.

| What they sign up for | You earn |
|---|---|
| Growth Engine or Enterprise, paid yearly | 25%, rising to 40% once you have signed more than ten |
| Foundation, paid yearly | 10% |
| Any plan, paid monthly | Nothing for their first three months, then 10% |

**The part worth understanding:** once you pass ten yearly Growth Engine or Enterprise members, you move to 40%, and your first ten move up with you when they renew. So the eleventh sale does not just pay more, it lifts everything you have already built.

**Yearly beats monthly, by a long way.** A business paying monthly earns you nothing for three months and then a small share. A business paying yearly earns you real money the moment their payment clears. Learn to sell the yearly plan and everything changes.

## Section 4: How and when you are paid

Commission goes into your account when the business's payment actually clears, not when they sign up.

It sits for 14 days, which covers reversed payments, then it is available.

We pay out every week. Once your available balance reaches R750, the whole balance goes to your bank account. If it takes a while to get to R750 it just carries over, and anything sitting longer than six months gets paid out no matter the amount.

If a business gets a refund after you have been paid, that amount comes off your next earnings. We will never send you an invoice or ask for money back.

## Section 5: What you get on day one

**Your own page.** Your name, your photo, your story, at your own web address. Not a link with a code on the end, an actual page you would be happy to send to anyone.

**A dashboard that tells you the truth.** Who clicked, who signed up, who is still on their free trial, who has paid, and who has not converted yet, with their contact details so you can follow them up.

**Ready made social posts.** Branded, with your name and your link, new ones every month. You do not have to think about what to post.

**Scripts for what to say.** What to open with. What to send on WhatsApp. What to say when they tell you they already have a Facebook page, because they will say that, and there is a good answer.

**Your own services on your page too.** If you already do something else, photography, design, printing, your page has a place for it. This does not have to be the only thing you do.

## Section 6: Who this works for

People who already talk to business owners all day. If you deliver, repair, supply, or sell to small businesses, you are having these conversations already.

People with a real local network in a town where nobody is doing this yet.

People who are comfortable walking into a shop and starting a conversation.

People who want something they can build up slowly, alongside whatever else they do, that keeps paying after the work is done.

## Section 7: Who this does not work for

We would rather be straight with you than waste your time.

**If you need money this month, this is not it.** Commission comes when a business pays, and businesses take time to decide. The first few weeks are usually quiet.

**If you were hoping to post a link and wait, this is not it.** The people who do well here talk to businesses directly. Posting alone almost never converts.

**If you want a salary, this is not it.** There is no basic, no guarantee, and no minimum.

## Section 8: Staying on the programme

One rule. Sign in to your dashboard at least once every 60 days.

There is no sales target and no quota. If you go quiet we warn you three times before anything happens, and if your account does close, everything you have already earned is still paid out to you.

Full detail is in the agent terms, and the FAQ answers the rest. Read both before you apply. We would rather you know exactly what this is.

## Section 9: How to apply

Fill in the form. We read every application and come back to you either way.

If you are accepted, you get your login, we set up your page together, and you can start the same week.

**Form fields (keep):** Full name, email, WhatsApp number, town or area, and one open question:
*Tell us how you would find your first three businesses.*

That single question tells you more than every tick box currently on the form, and it filters out anyone planning to spam.

**Remove from the form:** the Facebook page link, the two yes/no Facebook questions, and the promotion method dropdown. They point applicants at Facebook group posting, which converts badly and risks their Meta account and your brand.

**Remove urgently:** the "Secure payment via Paystack" line currently sitting under this form. On a page recruiting people who need income, it reads as though they must pay to join.
