# KatisoBiz Jobs: tester brief

**For somebody testing this for the first time, with no background.**
Written 9 August 2026, for the launch check on `jobs.katisobiz.co.za`.

Thank you for doing this. You are the most useful person in the process,
because you are the only one who has not already decided how it works.

---

## Before you start

**Use a real phone if you can.** Almost everybody who uses this will be on
one. If you only have a laptop, make the browser window narrow, about the
width of a phone, and use it that way.

**Use the real address: `jobs.katisobiz.co.za`.** Some of the security
checks only appear there, so testing anywhere else does not prove much.

**You need two email addresses you can actually open**, because signing up
sends you a code you have to type in. One for the job seeker side and a
different one for the employer side. Do not use the same one for both.

**Make up a person.** Do not use your own ID number, banking details or
anything private. You should never be asked for any of it. If you ever are,
that by itself is a serious finding, so write it down and tell us.

**Do not use any account somebody has already made.** Start fresh.

---

## What we actually want from you

Not a bug list. We want to know **where you hesitated.**

For each thing below, after you finish, tell us:

1. Did it do what you expected? If not, what did you expect?
2. Was there any point where you did not know what to do next, or had to
   read something twice?
3. Was there any point where you were not sure whether something had
   worked?
4. Anything that felt untrustworthy, or that you would not do with your
   real details?

"I got there in the end but it took me a second" is exactly the kind of
thing we want. So is "I have no idea what that word means."

Please do not look at the other tests before doing yours. Read the task,
then go.

---

## Test 1: I am looking for work and I have no CV

You are somebody who needs a job and has never had a CV.

Start at `jobs.katisobiz.co.za` and get yourself a CV.

Then: get a copy of it onto your phone or computer that you could email to
somebody.

Then: find a job on the site and apply for it.

Then: close everything, come back, and get back into your account.

**Tell us:** how long it took, anything you had to guess at, and whether at
the end you were confident an employer had actually received your
application.

---

## Test 2: I already have a CV, I do not want to type it all again

Same as above, but this time you already have a CV as a PDF or a Word file.
Use any old CV, your own or one you make up in Word in two minutes.

Start at `jobs.katisobiz.co.za` and get that CV into the system without
retyping it.

**Tell us:** how many questions you still had to answer after uploading,
whether what it read off your file was correct, and whether anything from
your file went missing.

---

## Test 3: the one we most want checked

This is the flow that was broken, so please be fussy here.

1. Make an account and finish a CV. Note the name you used.
2. **Log out.**
3. Now, still logged out, build or upload a **different** CV. Give this one
   a clearly different name so you can tell them apart.
4. **Log back in** to the account from step 1.

**Tell us:** what happened to each of the two CVs. Were you asked anything?
Did you end up with the one you wanted? Did anything disappear? Was it
clear at every point which CV you were looking at?

If at any point you could not tell which CV was which, say so. That is the
finding.

---

## Test 4: I am a business and I need to hire somebody

Use your **second** email address for this.

You run a small business and you need staff.

Go to `jobs.katisobiz.co.za`, set yourself up as an employer, and advertise
a job. Make up something ordinary: a driver, a cashier, a plumber.

Then, from a **different browser or a private window** (so you are not
logged in as the employer), apply for your own job as a job seeker, using
the seeker account from Test 1.

Then go back to the employer side and deal with that application: read
their CV, get a copy of it, and send them a message.

**Tell us:** whether you could find everything, whether you got an email
when the application arrived, and whether you would trust this enough to
actually hire through it.

---

## Test 5: the back and forth

Following on from Test 4, with both accounts:

- As the employer, message the applicant.
- As the seeker, read it and reply.
- As the seeker, change your mind and pull out of the job.
- As the employer, see what that looks like from your side.

**Tell us:** whether both sides always knew there was something waiting for
them, and whether the wording made sense in both directions.

---

## Test 6: poke at it

No script. Ten minutes of trying to break it or get lost.

Some things worth trying:

- Use the menu on every screen you can reach. Can you always get back? Can
  you always get out?
- Log out from a few different screens.
- Press Back a lot, in the browser and on the screens themselves.
- Refresh halfway through filling something in. Did you lose your work?
- Type nonsense into a form and submit it. Does it tell you what to do, or
  just complain?
- Look at a job advert. Is it easy to read? Do you know what the job is
  within five seconds?
- Try to find out what any of this costs you.
- Try to find a way to contact a real person.

---

## Things we already know, so do not spend time on them

- **The jobs board is nearly empty.** We have just opened, so there is very
  little on it. That is expected, not a bug.
- **The counters on the front page may show zero.** Also expected. Tell us
  if that put you off, though, because that is worth knowing.
- Payment for employers beyond the first free post is not part of this
  round.

---

## Please write down

- Which phone or computer and which browser you used.
- Roughly how long each test took.
- Anything you would not have worked out without asking somebody.

And please tell us the boring stuff too. A word that reads oddly, a button
you nearly missed, a screen that felt too busy. Those are the findings that
never come back once everyone has got used to the thing.
