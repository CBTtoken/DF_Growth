## WHAT DIFFERS FROM HER LOVABLE SITE, AND WHY

- **Structure, not just re-skinning.** Her site is thin on words, which
  is fine, not a defect to hide. The multipage feel on this platform
  comes from arrangement (a proper gallery, a packages section, a
  dedicated enquiry step) rather than from padding her copy with
  invented text.
- **Findability.** Real title, description, structured data and alt text
  on every image, none of which her original site carried in a form
  search engines could use well. This page beats the Lovable one
  specifically at being found, which was the brief.
- **No claim she hasn't made.** Her site's "in partnership with a
  professional décor company" line is kept, it's her own published
  claim. Her "20 years" experience figure and a "reply within 24 hours"
  promise were both left out, neither confirmed, and can go back in the
  moment she confirms them.

## THE EVENTS ARCHETYPE, FOR THE LIBRARY

What a call-out trade page (Fieldwork, built for Jetting Worx) does not
need and this one does: a photo-led hero people browse rather than act on
immediately, packages presented as options to compare rather than a
single service to book, and an enquiry as the primary action rather than
a phone number as the first thing a visitor sees. Full theme notes in
`docs/Theme_Library.md`.
