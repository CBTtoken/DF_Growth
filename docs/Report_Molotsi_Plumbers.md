## WHAT WAS BUILT

**A new theme, "Copperline" (`copperline`).** Built for the neighbourhood
trades of the informal market, where business starts with a WhatsApp
message. The first hero with WhatsApp as the primary action, in WhatsApp's
own green; calling second, the quote form third. The copper line (his own
material, all over his photos) threads the hero and turns the services into
junctions on a pipe run; his watermarked job photos hang as prints on a
wall; the address is said the way a neighbour would say it. Registered,
named, in the picker, available to every future member. Full entry in
`docs/Theme_Library.md`.

**His page**, written around his own sentence shape on purpose. The headline
is his own cadence: "We do plumbing. We do tiling. We do ceilings." Colours
sampled from his logo: copper and deep navy.

**His account**, on his existing login, comped free until **4 November
2026** with the reason on the record; the existing reminder crons watch that
date. Nothing routes through Paystack. His KatisoBiz grant was not touched,
per Dewald.

**Leads**: the page's lead form emails molotsiplumbers@gmail.com, and the
hero pushes visitors straight to his WhatsApp (067 954 7503) and phone, so
most contact will land where he already lives. **No automated email was
sent** so Dewald can send his own note.
