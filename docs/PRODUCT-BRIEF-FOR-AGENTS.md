# DigitalFlyer SA: what we sell, and what it does

**A brief for business agents and for writing marketing material.**

Everything in here was checked against the live code on 31 July 2026, not
against older specs. Where something is built but not finished, it says so, and
nothing in here should be promised to a member unless it appears below.

---

## The company

**Digital Flyer (Pty) Ltd**, trading as **DigitalFlyer SA**. We sell three
products to South African small and one-person businesses. They are separate
tools that work together, and a member can use one or all three.

| Product | The one-line pitch | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth** | Get found by local customers, and get them to phone you | growth.digitalflyersa.co.za |
| **KatisoBiz** | Quote and invoice from your phone in under a minute | katisobiz.co.za |
| **The Board** | A community notice board where members post work and offers | on Growth, at /board |

**How to position the three together.** KatisoBiz is how a business gets its
paperwork right. Growth is how it gets found. The Board is where the community
sees it. A tradesman usually arrives through KatisoBiz because it solves
today's problem, then buys Growth once he wants more work coming in.

# 1. GROWTH

## What the member actually gets

**A professional web page at `growth.digitalflyersa.co.za/their-name`.** It is
built from an onboarding form, not from a website builder they have to learn.
They answer questions about their business and the page is generated.

### On the page

- **A hero** with their business name, logo and headline
- **WhatsApp and Call buttons above the fold**, and again at the bottom, with
  their number shown as text so a customer on a desktop can read and copy it
- **An about section** in their own words
- **A services list**, each with a short description
- **Packages and pricing**, if they have them
- **A photo gallery** of their own work
- **Customer reviews**, with a star rating
- **A map** to their premises, or their service area in words where they have
  no fixed address
- **A contact form**, for enquiries after hours
- **Social links** to Facebook, Instagram and their own website

### Ten page designs

Members choose from ten templates, each with its own hero and section
arrangement: Single-Action Minimalist, Left-Heavy Split, Content-Dense Feature
Grid, Storyteller Vertical, High-Impact Dark Mode, Social Proof First,
Interactive Step-by-Step, Bold Geometric, Multi-Product Showcase, and App-Style
Checklist. The page uses the member's own brand colours throughout.

### Getting found

- **A place on the DigitalFlyer marketplace**, searchable by trade and town
- **Built for Google**: every page carries its own title, description, business
  structured data and a proper address, so it can rank for "plumber in
  Boksburg" rather than only for the business's own name
- **Fast**: pages are cached at the edge and carry no unnecessary scripts

### Knowing it works

- **A dashboard counter showing how many people got in touch this month**,
  split by WhatsApp, phone call and form, compared against last month
- **Page view tracking** with a seven-day trend
- **A lead inbox**: every form submission, with the ability to mark it handled
- **Meta Pixel and Conversions API** so a member running Facebook ads can see
  which ads produced enquiries

### Reviews

Customers leave a review in one screen: a rating, a comment and their name. No
account, no password, no email verification. Members get a direct link to send
to a customer after a job. Reviews can be replied to publicly. Fraudulent
reviews, such as one from the business's own network, are flagged and held for
a human.

### Booking and Shop, Growth tier and above

- **Booking**: publish bookable units or time slots with operating hours,
  buffers and a cancellation policy. Customers reserve from the page.
- **Shop**: list products with prices and stock, take orders, set flat or
  free-over delivery, and integrate with Bob Go for courier collection using
  the member's own Bob Go account.

### Also included at every tier

- **A monthly digital asset**, a ready-made graphic they can post anywhere
- **The KatisoBiz Nomads community**
- **KatisoBiz**, at the level set out under tiers below

# 2. KATISOBIZ

## What it is, in one sentence

A mobile-first tool that lets a service business build a quote in under sixty
seconds on a phone, send it on WhatsApp, turn it into an invoice with one tap,
and track who has paid.

**It is deliberately not an accounting package.** No ledgers, no payroll, no
VAT returns. It makes documents and tracks money. Say this plainly to
prospects: the boundary is a feature, and pretending otherwise loses trust the
first time someone asks about their books.

## What a member can do

**Quoting and invoicing**
- Build a quote on a phone, from a saved price list
- Send it on WhatsApp from their own number, or by email under their own
  business name with replies going to them
- Convert an accepted quote to an invoice with one tap
- Issue credit notes to correct a mistake in a way that is legally clean

**VAT, handled for them**
- Charges VAT correctly based on whether they are a registered vendor
- A non-vendor's documents carry the line "Not a VAT vendor. No VAT charged."
- No accounting vocabulary anywhere in the interface

**Getting paid**
- See who owes what, and how overdue it is
- Send a statement to a repeat customer, which is the tool for chasing money
- Their banking details printed correctly on every document
- Payment reminders

**Their business, properly presented**
- Their own logo on documents, on the paid plan
- Five document templates
- A saved customer database
- Reports, and a one-tap export pack for their accountant

**Also**
- Works on any phone with no app to install
- A free listing on Find a Trade, our public list of KatisoBiz members, where
  customers can search by trade and message them on WhatsApp

## KatisoBiz plans

| Plan | Price | Documents a month | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | **R0, forever** | 10 | One template, no logo upload, WhatsApp sending, VAT handling, credit notes |
| **KatisoBiz** | **R49/month** | 75 | All five templates, own logo, customer list, reports and statements, accountant export |
| **Unlimited** | **R89/month** | Unlimited | Everything above, with no monthly document limit |

**Top-ups:** another 75 documents for R49, and they never expire.

**Important on Unlimited:** multi-user and recurring invoices are **not built**.
They are a later sprint and every account is currently limited to one user.
**Do not sell Unlimited on those two features.** Sell it on document volume
only.

**Included with Growth:** Foundation members get the Free plan. Growth and
Enterprise members get the **R49 plan at no extra cost**. This applies whether
they pay monthly or annually. If a Growth subscription lapses, the KatisoBiz
entitlement drops back to Free and **nothing is deleted**.

# 3. THE BOARD

## What it is

A community notice board where DigitalFlyer members post to other members and
to the public. It is not a social network and there is no engagement scoring:
no likes, no view counts, no ranking by popularity. Posts are ordered by when
they were published, by area and by trade.

## What a member can post

Four kinds, all the same simple form:

- **An offer**, a special or a deal
- **For sale**, an item they are selling
- **An update**, news about the business
- **A job done**, finished work with a photo

Each post can carry a title, some words, a photograph and an optional price.

## How it works

- **Browse by area or by trade**, using the same trade categories as the
  marketplace, so a category means one thing across the platform
- **A post's area comes from the member's own town**, so a business that moves
  does not leave a trail of posts filed under the old one
- **Chat**, so someone who sees a post can message the member
- **Members of the public can post too**, with their own area
- **Moderation** for anything reported

**Status: built and live, but unlisted.** It is not yet promoted in the main
navigation. Check before telling a prospect it is available to them.

# 4. How the three connect

- **One login.** A member with both Growth and KatisoBiz signs in once and
  switches between them.
- **One company.** Everything is DigitalFlyer SA. KatisoBiz is not a separate
  brand, and correspondence always comes from DigitalFlyer SA.
- **The marketplace and Find a Trade are different things on purpose.**
  The marketplace is where paying Growth members are found, with full pages and
  reviews. Find a Trade is a thin free list of KatisoBiz members. Never call
  either of them a "directory" or a "listing", they are a marketplace.
- **KatisoBiz is the way in.** It is cheap, it solves a problem the member has
  today, and every document sent carries a small "Generated via KatisoBiz"
  line seen by another business owner.

# 5. Rules for anyone writing marketing material

1. **Never claim a feature that is not built.** The live example: multi-user
   and recurring invoices were on the KatisoBiz Unlimited card and neither
   exists.
2. **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Use a comma or a full stop.
3. **It is a marketplace.** Never a directory, never a listing.
4. **Open emails with "Good day {name},"**, never "Hi there".
5. **Plain South African English.** No "elevate", no "unlock", no jargon.
6. **Prices exactly as above.** Foundation R100/month or R900/year. Growth
   R180/month or R1,199/year. KatisoBiz R49 and R89.
7. **Enterprise is not for sale yet.**
8. **Do not invent results.** We do not publish customer counts, ratings or
   success statistics, because we would have to stand behind them.

# 6. Honest gaps, so nobody is caught out

These are real and a prospect may notice them.

- **Enterprise has no checkout.** It cannot be bought.
- **KatisoBiz Unlimited** is document volume only today.
- **The Board is unlisted** and gets little traffic.
- **Member pages are new and traffic is still small.** Do not promise a volume
  of leads. Sell the page, the marketplace presence and the tools, and be
  straight that traffic is something we are building together.
- **Prohibited industries.** Adult entertainment, gambling and multi-level
  marketing schemes require written permission before signing up. If a member
  is found to be in one of these after paying, the account is removed with no
  refund. Get this in front of a prospect early rather than late.
