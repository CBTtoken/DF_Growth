## How this sprint runs

### Permissions

**Run continuously. Do not stop to ask permission for anything except the deny
list below.** Dewald approves everything as it comes and does not read the
prompts, so asking slows delivery without adding safety. Assume yes for
everything not explicitly denied, including:

- Reading, writing, editing and creating files inside the project
- Running builds, installs, type checks, linters and tests
- Git add, commit and push to a working branch
- Crawling and fetching public web pages
- Fixing house style violations wherever found, without asking, every time

**Stop and ask before any of these, every time:**

- Deleting files or directories
- Force pushing, rewriting history, or pushing to the main branch
- Anything touching `.env.local` or any secret
- Anything that changes Vercel environment variables or deployment settings
- Anything that writes to the production database
- Anything touching Paystack, Resend or Meta credentials or webhooks

### When you need Dewald

He is not technical. When something genuinely needs him, do not ask
mid-stream. Collect it and give him one block of numbered, plain-language
steps: where to click, what to type, what he should see when it worked. Then
carry on with anything not blocked by it.

### Session continuity

If context runs short, stop cleanly rather than rushing the end of an item.
Write a short state file at the project root containing:

- Which of the three items are complete, in progress and untouched
- Exactly where the in-progress item stopped
- Anything discovered that changes what this document assumes
- A one paragraph resume prompt Dewald can paste into a fresh session

Tell him the state file exists and where it is.

## Item 2: build the Growth page for SIP Happens

### Who this is

SIP Happens Bespoke Mobile Bar. Contact: siphappens.mobilebar2025@gmail.com

Existing site: `https://sip-happens-web-magic.lovable.app/`

She approached DigitalFlyer directly. She works full time, is building the
business on the side, has little time and a small budget. She likes her current
site. She is getting one month of Growth free and will then decide whether to
continue.

### What to do

- Crawl her existing site properly, all pages, and take her content, her
  services, her wording and her images from it. It is her material and she has
  asked us to work from it.
- Build her page following the existing Growth Build Kit rules.
- **Do not attempt a pixel copy of the Lovable site.** She has been told
  plainly that this is a rebuild on our platform, not a copy. Our pages are
  generated from our own system.
- Her page must beat the Lovable one at the thing that actually matters: being
  found. Correct title and description carrying her trade and the areas she
  serves, proper structured data, real alt text on every image, and a
  legitimate address or service-area setup.

### The archetype point, which matters beyond this one build

She is the first **considered-purchase** business we have built for. Molotsi and
Jetting Worx are urgent call-out trades, where the visitor has a problem now and
wants a number in four seconds. An events customer browses, compares and looks
at pictures before deciding.

The page shape should reflect that: gallery-led, room to look, packages
visible, enquiry rather than emergency.

**Produce a reusable theme from this build and add it to the library**, named
so it is obvious it is the events and considered-purchase shape. Note in the
report what had to be different from the call-out shape, because that list is
the beginning of the section library we will build next.

### Watch for

- Her existing site is thin on words. That is fine. The multipage feel comes
  from arrangement, not from more content. Do not pad it with invented copy,
  and do not invent a fact she has not given us.
- Photo quality drives layout. If she has good photographs, lead with them. If
  there are only two usable images, choose a shape that does not expose that.
- Do not put "verified" anywhere. No verification mechanism exists.

### Needs Dewald

- Granting her free time on the account. Dewald does this himself from admin,
  so do not build anything for it. Just tell him when the page is ready so he
  can set it.
- Anything in her content that reads as a claim we would have to stand behind.
- Final look before it goes live, and her email address for the handover.
