# Part 1: Agent terms

*Last updated: [date]. These terms apply to everyone accepted into the DigitalFlyer SA Agent Programme.*

## 1. What an agent is

1.1 An agent introduces South African businesses to DigitalFlyer SA and earns commission when those businesses become paying members.

1.2 You are an independent contractor. You are not an employee, partner, or representative of DigitalFlyer SA, and you may not describe yourself as one. You set your own hours and choose how you work.

1.3 You are responsible for your own tax on everything you earn here. We do not deduct PAYE and we do not register you with SARS. If you are unsure what you owe, speak to a tax practitioner.

1.4 You may run your own business at the same time. Many agents do. Your business gets its own member page and dashboard, and your agent work gets its own agent page and dashboard, under the same login.

1.5 Applying does not guarantee acceptance. We review every application and may decline without giving a reason.

## 2. How a referral is counted as yours

2.1 You get a personal agent page with your own web address, and every link and button on it carries your referral code.

2.2 When someone reaches DigitalFlyer SA through your page or your link, that referral is held against your code for 30 days.

2.3 If they sign up within those 30 days, the business is attributed to you permanently, for as long as your account stays active.

2.4 If two agents have introduced the same business, the referral goes to whichever agent's link was used most recently before signup.

2.5 A business that signs up on its own, with no agent link, is a direct signup and earns no commission. We do not attribute businesses retrospectively.

## 3. What you earn

3.1 There are two rates. Which one applies depends on the plan the business takes.

**Growth Engine and Enterprise, paid annually**

| Your position | Your rate |
|---|---|
| Your first 10 annual members | 25% |
| From your 11th annual member onward | 40% |

**Everything else**

| Plan | Your rate |
|---|---|
| Foundation, paid annually | 10% |
| Any plan, paid monthly | Nothing for the first 3 months, then 10% |

3.2 Only Growth Engine and Enterprise annual members count toward your ten. Foundation and monthly members earn you commission but do not move you up the ladder.

3.3 Your count is cumulative for the life of your account. It never goes down, even if a member you signed cancels.

3.4 On monthly plans, the three unpaid months are counted as three cleared payments, not three calendar months. If a member pauses and comes back, their count carries on from where it stopped.

3.5 If a monthly member upgrades to an annual Growth Engine or Enterprise plan, they count toward your ten from that point, and you earn the annual rate that your count earns at that moment.

3.6 Commission is calculated on the amount paid excluding VAT.

## 4. Renewals

4.1 You keep earning on a member every year they renew, for as long as your account stays active.

4.2 Your rate is worked out fresh at every payment, based on how many annual members you have at that moment. It is not fixed at signup.

4.3 This means your first ten move up with you. If you sign your eleventh annual member and then your earlier members renew, those renewals pay 40%, not 25%.

4.4 It also works the other way. If you are still at ten or fewer when your members renew, those renewals pay 25%.

## 5. When and how you get paid

5.1 Commission is added to your ledger when a member's payment actually clears, not when they sign up.

5.2 Every amount is then held for 14 days before it becomes available. This covers reversed and disputed payments.

5.3 We run a payout every week. If your available balance is R750 or more, the full available balance is paid out.

5.4 If your balance stays under R750, it carries over. Any balance that has been sitting for longer than 6 months is paid out in full at the next run, whatever the amount.

5.5 Payment goes by bank transfer to the account you have saved with us. Keeping those details current is your responsibility.

5.6 Your dashboard shows every amount at every stage, pending, clearing, available, and paid, with the member and date behind each one.

5.7 If a payment we have already paid you commission on is later reversed or refunded, that amount is deducted from your next earnings. We will not invoice you for it or ask you to pay it back.

## 6. Staying active

6.1 To stay active, sign in to your agent dashboard at least once every 60 days. That is the only requirement.

6.2 If you have not signed in, we will warn you at 30 days, again at 45 days, and a final time at 55 days, using every contact detail we hold for you.

6.3 If you still have not signed in by day 60, your account is deactivated.

## 7. What happens if your account is deactivated

7.1 Your agent page and your referral link stop working.

7.2 You stop earning on your members, including on renewals. Those businesses become direct members of DigitalFlyer SA.

7.3 Everything you have already earned is still yours. Your available balance is paid out at the next weekly payout, whatever the amount, and anything still clearing is paid once it clears.

7.4 You may apply to rejoin. You start again from zero, and businesses you introduced before are not returned to you.

## 8. Money we cannot pay you

8.1 If a payout fails, or we have no valid bank details for you, the amount is held for 90 days.

8.2 During those 90 days we will try to reach you on every contact detail we hold, and we keep a record of every attempt.

8.3 You can claim it at any point in those 90 days by giving us working bank details.

8.4 After 90 days, an unclaimed balance is forfeited.

8.5 If an agent has died, this clause does not apply. The account is frozen and the balance is settled with the estate.

## 9. How you may work

9.1 Represent DigitalFlyer SA honestly. Do not promise results we have not promised, and do not invent features.

9.2 Quote our published prices only. Do not advertise your own prices, discounts, or packages for DigitalFlyer SA products.

9.3 You may sell your own separate services alongside this, and your agent page has a place for them, as long as it is clear which is which.

9.4 No spam. That includes bulk unsolicited messaging, buying contact lists, and posting in groups against their rules. Meta bans accounts for this, and it lands on our brand as well as yours.

9.5 You will see the contact details of businesses attributed to you, so you can follow up with them. Those details belong to DigitalFlyer SA and are shared with you under POPIA for that purpose only. You may not sell them, share them, or use them for anything else, and you must delete them if your account closes.

9.6 We may deactivate an account immediately for a serious breach of this section, without the warning ladder in section 6.

## 10. Changes

10.1 We may change these terms. We will tell you at least 30 days before a change takes effect.

10.2 Commission already earned or clearing is paid at the rate that applied when it was earned.

10.3 If you do not accept a change, you may close your account, and section 7 applies.

# Part 2: Public FAQ

*For the agent recruitment page and the main FAQ. Same rules, plain language, written for someone deciding whether to apply.*

**What does an agent actually do?**
You introduce South African businesses to DigitalFlyer SA. When one of them becomes a paying member, you earn a share of what they pay, every year they stay.

**Do I need my own business to become an agent?**
No. You do not need to be a member yourself and you do not need a business. Plenty of agents do have their own business, and that is fine too. You get a page and a dashboard for each.

**Does it cost anything to join?**
No. There is no joining fee, no monthly fee, and nothing to buy. If anyone tells you otherwise, it is not us.

**What do I actually get?**
Your own page with your name, your photo and your story, on your own web address that you can share anywhere. A dashboard showing who signed up, who is still on trial and who has paid. Ready made social posts. Scripts for what to say. And the contact details of the businesses you brought in, so you can follow them up.

**How much can I earn?**
On a Growth Engine or Enterprise member paying annually, you earn 25% of what they pay. Once you have signed more than ten of those, every one earns 40%, including the first ten when they renew. Foundation members paying annually earn you 10%. Anyone paying monthly earns you nothing for their first three months and 10% after that.

**Why is monthly worth so much less?**
Monthly members cost us more to carry and leave sooner. Annual members stay. The rates are built to point you at the sale that is worth more to both of us.

**Do I get paid again next year?**
Yes. Every year a member you introduced renews, you earn on it again, for as long as your account is active.

**When do I get my money?**
Commission is added to your account once the member's payment clears, held for 14 days, then paid out at the next weekly payout as long as your balance is R750 or more. If it takes a while to reach R750, it carries over, and anything sitting longer than six months is paid out regardless.

**Why R750?**
Bank transfer costs make paying out very small amounts wasteful. The six month rule is there so nothing is ever stuck.

**What if someone cancels or gets a refund?**
That amount comes off your next earnings. We never ask you to pay money back.

**What do I have to do to stay active?**
Sign in to your dashboard at least once every 60 days. That is all. There is no sales target. If you have not signed in, we warn you three times before anything happens.

**What happens if I stop?**
If you go 60 days without signing in, your page and link stop working and you stop earning on future renewals. Everything you have already earned is still paid to you.

**Can I say I work for DigitalFlyer SA?**
No. You are independent, and you should say so. You are an agent, not an employee, and you handle your own tax.

**Can I set my own prices?**
No. Quote our published prices. You are welcome to sell your own separate services alongside, and your page has a section for exactly that.

**How do you know a signup came from me?**
Your page and your link carry your own code. Anyone who arrives through it is held against your name for 30 days, and if they sign up in that time they are yours permanently.

**How do I apply?**
Fill in the form on the agent page. We look at every application and come back to you.
