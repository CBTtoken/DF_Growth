## WHAT MOXIE IS

Moxie Magazine is South Africa's family discovery magazine.

- **Full name:** Moxie Magazine
- **Tagline:** Have the Moxie.
- **Website:** moxiemag.co.za
- **Contact:** info@moxiemag.co.za
- **Format:** Monthly digital PDF flipbook, published 1st of each month
- **Price:** R49/month standalone. Free with Smart Value Club membership.
- **Audience:** Curious minds aged 8 to 80. Primary reader: adults 28-55, urban and suburban, predominantly mobile, 62% female / 38% male.
- **Parent brand:** Smart Value Club (SVC) / smartvalueclub.co.za

Moxie is not a women's magazine. Not a kids' magazine. Not a news magazine. It is a magazine for the whole South African family — science, nature, history, travel, food, faith, puzzles, and real South African stories that do not appear anywhere else.

## EDITORIAL DNA — THE MOXIE VOICE

Warm, curious, and genuinely human. Like a conversation with someone who finds the world interesting. Clean, honest stories about adventures, experiences, and the things that bring families closer together.

**We lead with mission, never money. We are never the hero. We never hype, never condescend, never manufacture emotion.**

Always writing for two readers simultaneously: a curious 12-year-old AND a curious 50-year-old. Both should feel the article was written for them.

Articles are tight enough to hold attention and alive enough to make the reader want to get up and do something. Always leaving the reader with a warm "I want to be better and I want to be part of changing my community" feeling.

**Never:** Crime, politics, catastrophe, corporate tone, condescension, gambling, tobacco, alcohol targeting families, MLMs, get-rich-quick, anything conflicting with honesty, curiosity, and South African pride.

**CTAs:** One per page closing maximum. Links and contact info for people and organisations. Never forced.

## PRODUCTION WORKFLOW — FOLLOW THIS EVERY EDITION

1. **Write** — Draft all editorial content with Claude. Sign off each section before moving to design.
2. **PDF proof** — Claude builds PDF-ready pages using Python/ReportLab. Review and approve.
3. **Adobe Express** — Load approved copy. Place text and images.
4. **Export** — Print-ready PDF (300dpi, RGB) and eMag-ready PDF for flipbook.
5. **Publish** — Upload to flipbook platform, update website, send to SVC members.

No page goes to Adobe Express until copy is approved. Design time is expensive. Revision time is more expensive.

**PDF naming:** Moxie_Edition[XX]_[Month][Year].pdf

## LESSONS FROM EDITION 1 — APPLY FROM DAY ONE IN EDITION 2

**Layout:**
- Single column body copy always. No two-column editorial body, ever.
- Hero bands are always dark brown (#1E2020). No other dark colour.
- White space gaps larger than 20mm signal a problem. Close them.
- Moxie Tip box is always full width, never a side column.
- One CTA per page closing maximum.

**Typography:**
- Remove ALL placeholder text before building the PDF. Every word on every page must be real.
- Team bio cards must have identical font treatment across all four entries.
- No watermark text behind body copy.

**Contents page:**
- Every TOC entry is a complete unit: page number + pillar + title + subtitle, all connected.
- No large white space gaps between entries.

**Advertorial:**
- No grey placeholder boxes in the final PDF. Either the image is there or redesign the space.
- Consistent font sizes across all sections.

**Back cover:**
- MOXIE logotype at maximum 60% of page width.
- "Have the Moxie." in Playfair Display Italic, burnt orange, is the permanent sign-off.
- Next edition teaser in dark green block is mandatory.
