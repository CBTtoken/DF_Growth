## 2. THE MOXIE VOICE

Warm, curious, and genuinely human. Like a conversation with someone who finds the world interesting.

Every article is written for two readers at once: a curious 12-year-old and a curious 50-year-old. Both must feel the piece was written for them.

### How to write for Moxie

- **Lead with the story.** The person, the place, the discovery. Not the context, not the history. Start in the middle of something happening.
- **Never be the hero.** Moxie is never the hero. The story is. The person in the story is.
- **Earn every paragraph.** If a paragraph does not move the story forward, it does not go in.
- **End with something to carry.** Not a summary, not a moral. A feeling. Something the reader is still thinking about at dinner.
- **Write tight, not thin.** Tight means no wasted words. Thin means the meaning has been cut too.
- **South African, always.** Reference points, idiom, spelling. If you would not say it in a Joburg kitchen, reconsider it.

### Language rules, non-negotiable

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| No em dashes | Never use the long dash character. Use a comma, a full stop, or rewrite. Applies to articles, briefs, headings, captions, and every document. |
| No exclamation marks | If the sentence needs one to feel exciting, the sentence is not exciting enough. Rewrite it. |
| No sentences starting with "I" | Applies to all Moxie copy. |
| No double line breaks | Single paragraph space only. |
| No passive voice | Where active voice is possible, use it. |
| South African English | Colour not color. Realise not realize. Programme not program. |
| No AI phrases | delve into · it is important to note · at its core · tapestry of · moreover · furthermore · in summary · it is worth noting · navigate |
| No corporate jargon | leverage · synergy · ecosystem · stakeholder · impactful · going forward · holistic |
| One CTA per page | One call to action at a page closing, maximum. Never mid-article. Never forced. |

### Subject matter we never carry

Crime · politics · catastrophe · negativity · condescension · manufactured emotion.

Afrikaans reader content is published completely unedited and verbatim.

## 7. MANDATORY DESIGN DEVICES

Five devices appear in every edition. They are not optional.

### 01 · Four-column fact grid
**Appears on:** Cover Story opener page only.
Four equal columns at 45.5mm each (182 ÷ 4). Charcoal background. Inside each column from top: kicker label in Barlow Condensed Bold orange uppercase, display word or number in Playfair Display Bold white, descriptor in Barlow Condensed grey.

### 02 · Stat block
**Appears on:** The Quiet Hero, every edition, no exceptions.
Four equal columns at 45.5mm each. Large number in Barlow Condensed Bold 36-48pt orange. Descriptor in Source Serif 4 Regular 9-10pt white on charcoal, two to three lines below the number. Thin 0.5pt vertical rules between columns. Sits immediately below the standfirst, before body copy begins.

### 03 · Moxie Tip box
**Appears on:** Near the bottom of the final page of every article.
Background deep teal `#0B6E6E`. Left accent strip burnt orange, 3mm wide. Label "MOXIE TIP" in Barlow Condensed Bold 12pt light mint. Body in Source Serif 4 Italic 12pt white. Minimum height 22mm. **Full content width always. Never a side column. Never omitted.**

### 04 · Pull quote
**Appears on:** Every two-page article, at least one.
Playfair Display Italic 14-16pt, orange or charcoal, with a 3pt left vertical rule in teal or orange.

### 05 · Writer credit
**Appears on:** Every contributed article.
Photo of the writer, "WRITTEN BY" in Barlow Condensed Bold orange, name in Playfair Display, bio in Source Serif 4 Italic. External writers' voices are preserved entirely. Rewrites keep the original structure and tone.

## 9. ADVERTISING FORMATS

Advertisers supply print-ready artwork. Moxie does not design ads. Advertisers use their own brand colours and do not need to match the Moxie palette.

### Full page
- Trim: 210 x 297mm
- Bleed: 216 x 303mm (3mm all sides)
- Safe zone: 200 x 287mm (5mm inside trim)
- Placement: OBC · IFC · IBC · ROM
- Bleed required. Background must extend to the bleed line.

### Half page
- Horizontal: 210 x 148.5mm trim · 216 x 151.5mm bleed
- Vertical: 105 x 297mm trim · 108 x 303mm bleed
- Editorial adjacent, shares a page with editorial content
- Bleed required

### Quarter page
- Trim: 105 x 148.5mm
- No bleed required, supply at trim size
- Safe zone 5mm inside all edges
- Two quarter pages share one full page, paired with a compatible advertiser

### Placement positions
| Code | Position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| OBC | Outside Back Cover | Most premium position |
| IFC | Inside Front Cover | First thing readers see |
| IBC | Inside Back Cover | High dwell time |
| ROM | Run of Magazine | Between sections, at Moxie discretion |

### Submission
- File format: PDF/X-1a or JPG, RGB, 300dpi minimum
- All fonts embedded or outlined. JPG supply must be flattened.
- Email only to editor@moxiemag.co.za · subject line: advertiser name · edition month · format
- No WhatsApp or messaging apps. They compress files.
- Deadline: 10th of the month prior to publication
- Artwork below spec is returned

### Standing inventory per edition
2 advertorials · 2 full pages · 3 half pages · 4 quarter pages

### What we do not accept
Gambling · tobacco · alcohol targeting families · multi-level marketing · get-rich-quick products · misleading claims · unsubstantiated comparative advertising · anything conflicting with honesty, curiosity, and South African pride.

## 10. LAYOUT RULES, ABSOLUTE

- Single column body copy always. No two-column editorial body text, ever.
- Paragraphs never split across a page break. Push the whole paragraph to the next page.
- No widows or orphans. Fix stranded single lines before the page goes to design.
- No white space gap larger than 20mm between elements.
- Moxie Tip box is always full content width. Never a side column.
- One CTA per page closing, maximum.
- Hero bands are always charcoal `#1E2020`. The only exception is the BELIEVE hero band, which is teal.
- Buttons and boxes are sharp rectangles. Border radius 0. Everywhere.
- No placeholder text on any final or client-facing page.
- Every section opener needs a strong photograph.
- Captions below every image, always.
- Pull quote in every two-page article.
- Never mix the SVC and Moxie colour palettes in the same document.

## 11. PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

The order never changes.

1. **Write.** Draft all editorial content in chat. One chat per section or article, not one chat per edition.
2. **Approve.** Every article approved in chat before any PDF is exported. Words first, always.
3. **Layout brief.** For each approved section, produce a brief covering text hierarchy, design devices required, image direction, and element placement.
4. **Design.** Load approved copy and brief into Adobe Express at 210 x 297mm.
5. **Assemble.** Add images, confirm final page numbers.
6. **Contents last.** Build the TOC only once final page numbers are confirmed.
7. **Export.** RGB PDF at 300dpi. Named `Moxie_Edition[XX]_[Month][Year].pdf`. No placeholder text on any page.
8. **Publish.** Upload to the flipbook platform, update moxiemag.co.za, send to SVC members on the 1st.

**Content deadline:** Thursday lunchtime before publication date.
**Artwork deadline:** 10th of the prior month.

### Tooling

| Stage | Tool |
|---|---|
| Copy and approval | Chat |
| PDF drafts for approval | Python ReportLab |
| Client and subject approval documents | Word first, then PDF layout |
| Final layout and assembly | Adobe Express, 210 x 297mm canvas |
| Visual verification | PyMuPDF / fitz, 3x scale PNG previews |
| Content verification | pdfplumber |
| Image processing | Pillow, centre-crop with 0.15 to 0.30 top fraction |

### ReportLab build notes

- Register all four Moxie fonts at the start of every build session, before any other code runs. Without this, ReportLab silently falls back to Helvetica and every measurement is wrong.
- Variable fonts from Google Fonts need fonttools instancer extraction to static instances first. Source Serif 4 Regular at `opsz=12, wght=400`. Playfair Display Bold at `wght=700`.
- Pre-measure all content heights before building. Building without pre-measurement causes overflow or white space gaps.
- Set `wordWrap='LTR'` on every Paragraph style to prevent overflow on Afrikaans and other non-ASCII characters.
- Pass styled text as (label, body) tuples rather than HTML bold markup. Markup fails silently when the bold variant is not registered.
- Scan every output with pdfplumber for leaked markup tags before delivery.
- ReportLab output is for approval drafts only. Never final output.

## 12. WEBSITE ALIGNMENT

The site runs WordPress with OceanWP, Elementor, and WooCommerce, and uses the same palette and type system as the magazine.

- Header border-bottom, top bar background, footer background: charcoal `#1E2020`
- Primary buttons and links: burnt orange `#C85A1E`
- SVC cross-sell banner: deep teal `#0B6E6E` with an orange R2 000+ badge. Mandatory on the homepage, the Subscribe page, and every blog article page.
- Nav and UI: Barlow Condensed Bold uppercase, 1.5px letter spacing
- Headings: Playfair Display Bold. Body: Source Serif 4.
- Button border radius: 0 globally.
- Product cards are generated by WooCommerce and styled through the Customizer. Never rebuilt in Elementor.

### Social media

Facebook is the primary platform.

- Never put the moxiemag.co.za link in the post body. Drop it as the first comment immediately after publishing, because Facebook suppresses reach on posts with outbound links.
- Weekdays: two posts, 07:00 and 19:30.
- Weekends and public holidays: one post, 09:00.
- Emojis required. Tone warm and human, matching the magazine voice.
- Calendars delivered as Word documents, copy-and-paste ready, in a three-part format: creative brief with Canva image text, then post copy with hashtags and emojis, then date and time to post.
