# MOXIE MAGAZINE — PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS

## What Moxie Is

**Full name:** Moxie Magazine
**Tagline:** Have the Moxie.
**Definition:** South Africa's boldest family discovery magazine
**Website:** moxiemag.co.za
**Format:** Monthly digital PDF flipbook, published 1st of each month
**Pricing:** Free with SVC membership / R49/month standalone
**Audience:** Curious minds 8–80. Primary reader: adults 28–55, urban and suburban, predominantly mobile, predominantly female
**Editorial formula:** Discovery as the engine. Humanity as the heartbeat.

Moxie is the editorial sister brand of Smart Value Club (SVC) — a separate Project. SVC members receive Moxie free as part of their membership. This is listed in SVC T&Cs. The relationship: SVC is the engine. Moxie is the heart. Neither exists to hype the other.

## Production Workflow — Follow This Sequence Every Edition

1. **Write** — Draft all editorial content in the edition chat. Each section written, reviewed, and signed off before moving to design.
2. **Layout brief** — For each completed section, Claude provides a layout brief: text hierarchy, design devices, image direction.
3. **Adobe Express** — Load approved copy and layout brief. Place text into locked template. Source and place images.
4. **Export** — Print-ready PDF (300dpi, RGB) and eMag-ready PDF (optimised for digital flipbook).
5. **Publish** — Upload to flipbook platform, update Moxie website, send to SVC members.

**Rule:** No page goes to Adobe Express until copy is approved in the edition chat. Design time is expensive. Revision time is more expensive.

## Advertising — Three Formats

Moxie accepts three formats. Advertisers supply print-ready artwork — Moxie does not design ads. Artwork deadline: 10th of the month prior to publication.

**Full page:** 210 × 297mm trim / 216 × 303mm bleed / 200 × 287mm safe zone / PDF/X-1a or JPG / RGB / 300dpi min
Positions: IFC (Inside Front Cover) · IBC (Inside Back Cover) · OBC (Outside Back Cover — most premium) · Run of Magazine

**Half page horizontal:** 210 × 148.5mm trim / 216 × 151.5mm supply size
**Half page vertical:** 105 × 297mm trim / 108 × 303mm supply size
Placement: Editorial adjacent, shares page with editorial content

**Quarter page:** 105 × 148.5mm trim / no bleed required / 5mm safe zone / two per page
Placement: Paired with compatible advertiser

**All formats:** RGB only / PDF/X-1a or high-res JPG / 300dpi minimum / fonts embedded or outlined

**Rates:** Complimentary for Edition 1. To be confirmed from Edition 2 onwards.

**Will not carry:** Gambling, tobacco, alcohol targeting families, MLM schemes, get-rich-quick products, anything conflicting with editorial values.
